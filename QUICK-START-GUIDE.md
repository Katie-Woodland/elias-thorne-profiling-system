# Quick-Start Guide  
**Dr. Elias Thorne Psychological Profiling System**

**Version 2026-05-11**  
Created by Katie Woodland (@wookat1983)

### Why use this system?
Most people unconsciously assume others think and act like themselves. This tool helps you **see your own biases first**, then gives you a clear Trustworthiness Index + bias map to use with your Heuer matrix (Analysis of Competing Hypotheses, Judgement Matrix, etc.).

### Step-by-step: How to use it

**Step 1 – Profile YOURSELF first (most important)**
1. Open the file `Dr-Elias-Thorne-Psychological-Profiling-System.md`
2. Copy the entire **MASTER PROMPT** (everything from the top down to the end).
3. Paste it into Grok, Claude, GPT-4o or any capable LLM.
4. At the start of your message, supply your own evidence:
   - Your full name and handle
   - Key life events, traumas, achievements
   - Any clinical notes, therapy transcripts, journals, or statements you want checked
5. Send the message. The AI will produce your full personal profile + **“Personal Bias & Projection Check for Heuer Use”** section.

**Step 2 – Use your own profile as a calibration lens**
When you run Heuer analysis on any external event, leader, or situation:
- Read the **Integration Note for Heuer-Based Analysis** and the **Personal Bias & Projection Check** from your self-profile.
- Actively test whether your own developmental wiring (e.g. high need for autonomy, aversion to control, etc.) is unconsciously weighting one hypothesis over another.
- This dramatically reduces the “assuming others think/act like ourselves” error.

**Step 3 – Profile others or events**
Use the same Master Prompt, but supply:
- The person’s full name and public handle
- Any specific claims, speeches, or statements you want
